Your agents

Use ChatGPT, Claude or another agent? Connect it to Bud: it shows its work on Bud’s face and waits for your tap before it acts.

Add the link to your agent as a custom MCP connector, with no sign-in. Anyone with the link can show things on Bud and ask you questions, but only you can answer them. A new link turns the old one off.

The link belongs to this browser. To answer on your phone with the same link, use “Use on another device” and open what it copies there. Keep that one private: it can answer for you.
