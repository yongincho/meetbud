What is Bud?

A little round screen with a face that snaps onto the back of your phone, with its own mic and speaker. Tap it and talk to your AI, and glance at it to see what your AI is doing.

Which phones does it work with?

iPhone today, with the Bud app in beta. Android is planned.

Is it always listening?

Only if you turn on “Hey Bud.” Otherwise it listens when you tap. Double-tap Bud to turn its mic off.

When can I get one?

Bud is a working prototype. People on the waitlist hear first about early units, price and timing.
