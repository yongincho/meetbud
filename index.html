Privacy

Mic on, light on.

Bud is designed with a small light wired to its microphone, like the camera light on a laptop. When the mic has power, the light is on, and no software can switch it off.

  • Listens when you tap.

    Bud is designed to listen when you ask it to, not before.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    What is Bud?

    A small round device with a screen that sits on the back of your phone. Inside is an AI agent you talk to. Tap it, ask for something, and Bud works on it, showing you its progress with its face.

    What can it do?

    The everyday stuff: scheduling, reminders, messages, reservations, lists and quick answers. Which apps and services it works with at launch is TBD.

    Will it work with my phone?

    Bud is being designed for both iPhone and Android phones. Final compatibility is TBD.

    How does it attach?

    With magnets. It snaps onto the back of your phone, below the camera, and comes off when you want. Details for phones without built-in magnets are TBD.

    How do I see what Bud is doing?

    Flip your phone. Bud’s screen rests to save battery and wakes up when you flip to it or tap it.

    Is it always listening?

    No. Flipping your phone wakes Bud’s face, and it listens only when you tap it. Bud is designed with a light wired to its mic, so you can see whenever the mic is on.

    Does it work with the AI I already use?

    That is the plan. Bud is built to be the face of your agent, whichever one you use. Which agents it connects to at launch is TBD.

    Is the deposit refundable?

    Yes. The deposit is fully refundable. You will see the full terms at checkout.

    Can I change how Bud looks?
